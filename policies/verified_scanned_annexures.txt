[Source: 03_Cancellation_and_Refund_Policy_v2.pdf, page 2]
Annexure 1: Approved refund schedule, Version 2.
Applies to bookings made on or after 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST.
Cancellation before scheduled pickup:
48 hours or more: 100% refund of rental charge.
12 hours or more, but less than 48 hours: 75%.
2 hours or more, but less than 12 hours: 25%.
Less than 2 hours: 0%.
Cancelled by RideWise: 100% refund in all cases.
No-show: no refund.
The refund percentage applies to the rental charge paid; GST on the refunded amount is refunded proportionally.

Annexure A: Approved late-return grace periods.
STANDARD: no grace period.
SILVER: no grace period.
GOLD: up to and including 30 minutes late incurs no late fee.
GOLD return more than 30 minutes late: full lateness is charged from the first minute.
